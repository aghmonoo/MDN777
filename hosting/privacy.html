Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Staff Connect is a private human-resources application provided by MDN777 to its own staff. Accounts are created by an administrator; the app is not open to public sign-up.

Information we hold

  • Account — username, staff ID, display name, role, department, position, join date.
  • Payroll — payslips issued to you, and the bank name, account number and account holder name you enter for salary payment.
  • Documents — files you or your administrator upload to your profile.
  • Messages — group chat and direct messages, including any images or files sent.
  • Activity — which announcements you have read, and a log of changes made to your bank details.

The app does not collect location data, contacts, or device identifiers for advertising, and it contains no advertising.

How the information is used

Solely to operate the app for the employer: to show you your payslips and announcements, to let you and the HR team communicate, to keep your payroll details current, and to keep an audit trail of payroll changes.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own profile, payslips, documents and messages.
  • Administrators (the HR team) can see staff profiles, payslips, bank details and messages, as part of their work.
  • Other employees cannot see your payslips, bank details or documents.

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase — sign-in, database and hosting. See the Firebase privacy information.
  • Cloudinary — storage and delivery of uploaded images and documents. See the Cloudinary privacy policy.

Retention

Records are kept for as long as your employment relationship and the employer's record-keeping obligations require. Items deleted by an administrator are held in a recycle bin for 30 days before permanent deletion.

Security

Access requires a password. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and access is restricted by server-side security rules, so that each person can only read what their role permits.

Your choices

You can change your password and your bank details in the app. To correct other details, or to ask what is held about you, contact your HR administrator.

Children

The app is for employees only and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
