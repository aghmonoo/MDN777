Put it on your phone

Installing it to the home screen is what turns on notifications for new announcements, messages and payslips.

Android

  1. Open the web app in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install on the banner at the bottom — or the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.
  3. Open it from the home screen and allow notifications.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open the web app in Safari (not Chrome).
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the home screen and allow notifications.

Notifications need iOS 16.4 or newer, and only work once it is on the home screen.
